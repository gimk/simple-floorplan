## Design suggestions

1. **Fix `W14:L` outside chain vs overall mismatch**: Check why `W14:L` computes chain segment `214` while overall reports `221` in `src/features/rooms/outside/outsideDims.ts`.
2. **Hide or simplify room tag and wall dims in tiny rooms (`Wc`, `Dégagement`, `Sdb`)**: When a room's inner bounding box is smaller than the tag + dim line offset, suppress extra tag rows or hide inner wall dims below a higher screen-pixel threshold.
3. **Offset wall number badges away from dimension lines**: Place `[data-wall-number]` badges closer to the wall face or outside the dimension line offset so they do not collide with `[data-dim="wall"] text` (19 overlaps observed).
4. **Move short outside-dim text (`77`, `93`, `40`, `66`) clear of extension lines**: When an outside segment is narrower than its label, place the text outside the extension lines with a short leader instead of centering it on the ticks.
5. **Offset selected-wall `A`/`B` badge from the thickness box**: When `data-helper-thickness` is shown in the middle of the wall band, shift the `A` side badge slightly along the wall so it does not touch the thickness box.
6. **Avoid placing helper dim text directly on top of room wall dim text**: Offset `[data-helper-dim]` slightly further or suppress the duplicate room dim on the selected wall while helpers are visible.
7. **Restore or expose the Light/Dark theme switch**: Ensure `prefers-color-scheme: light` and `localStorage` `"simple-floorplan:theme"` apply `data-theme="light"` cleanly on load.
8. **Add `id` or `name` attributes to form inputs**: Resolves the 19 DevTools form-field accessibility issues.
